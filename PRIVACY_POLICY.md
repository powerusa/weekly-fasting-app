# Weekly Fasting App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

Weekly Fasting App is designed to be privacy-first. All fasting data is stored only on your device. No account is required and no personal data is collected.

## Information We Collect

Weekly Fasting App does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.

The app stores fasting plans, timer history, settings, and notification preferences locally on your device using Apple system storage.

## Local Data Storage

Your fasting data remains on your device. The app does not use a server, login system, analytics service, advertising SDK, or cloud database.

If you delete the app, locally stored app data may be deleted by iOS.

## Notifications

Weekly Fasting App may send local notifications for fasting events, such as when a fast starts, is almost finished, or is completed.

These notifications are created on your device and are not sent through our servers.

## Health Disclaimer

Weekly Fasting App is for tracking and educational purposes only. It is not medical advice. Always consult your doctor before fasting or changing your diet.

## Children's Privacy

Weekly Fasting App is not intended to collect information from children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

## Third-Party Services

Weekly Fasting App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

## Data Sharing

Weekly Fasting App does not share personal data because it does not collect personal data.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app changes. Any updates will be posted with a new effective date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the app developer through the support contact listed on the App Store.
